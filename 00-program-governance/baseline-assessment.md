## Diagnostic questions

Rate each from 0-4 and justify with one concrete example.

1. Can I choose ML vs GenAI vs RAG vs agentic architecture from business constraints?
2. Can I define measurable model and system evaluation thresholds?
3. Can I design private, auditable Bedrock integration patterns?
4. Can I architect retrieval with tenant-aware authorization and citation reliability?
5. Can I define tool permission boundaries and human approval controls for agents?
6. Can I produce an AI threat model that drives technical controls?
7. Can I design rollback, incident, and runbook patterns for AI operations?
8. Can I explain cost-per-query/cost-per-task tradeoffs and mitigations?
9. Can I defend architecture decisions through ADRs and security review?
10. Can I present portfolio artifacts for stakeholder sign-off?

## Baseline risk classification

| Risk level | Rule |
|---|---|
| High | Any of: average score < 2.0, or any critical dimension <= 1 |
| Medium | Average 2.0-2.9, no critical dimension <= 1 |
| Low | Average >= 3.0 with evidence-backed examples |

**Critical dimensions:** AI security, Responsible AI, evaluation design, platform architecture.
